# The Cobra Effect
hook: Paradox

VO: So the British paid people to kill *cobras.* / And they got more cobras.
BILD: Cobra rises big, coin drops, counter of cobras goes UP, title slams in

## s2 Setup
VO: // Delhi, British India. The story goes: way too many cobras. / So the plan was simple: bring in a dead cobra, get *cash.*

VO: // And at first? It works great. / The cobras disappear, and the officials *celebrate.*

VO: // But then people do the math. / Why hunt cobras when you can just *breed* them?

VO: // So, cobra farms. Like, in the backyard. / Business is *booming.*

## s7 Result
