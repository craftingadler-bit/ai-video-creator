# What if the Roman Empire never fell?
hook: What if + cliché escalation

## s1 Hook
VO: What if the *Roman Empire* never fell? / Like, never. Not even a little.
BILD: Columns, laurel wreath, SPQR, title slams in

## s2 The map
VO: // Then Rome owns all of Europe, North Africa, and half of Asia. / And the capital? / *Rome.* Shocking. Truly nobody saw that coming.
BILD: Real map, regions fill red word by word, star on Rome

## s3 Germania
VO: // Germany doesn't exist. / You live in *Germania.* A province. / Literally. Population: twelve people and a lot of cows.

## s6 Train
